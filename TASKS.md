## Work plan

1. Open and study each public product page.
2. Extract only public facts visible on the product page: title, positioning, format, target audience, benefits, price if public, and purchase URL.
3. Do not invent product contents that are not visible on the source page.
4. Group products into topic clusters for future SEO structure.
5. Propose human-readable slugs for product review pages.
6. Identify supporting SEO article topics for each cluster.
7. After study, create or update site content only in `goodly-geland`.
