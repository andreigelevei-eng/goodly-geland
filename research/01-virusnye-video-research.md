## Market context, not product claims

The broader market confirms that short-form video is an important marketing format, but these external facts must not be rewritten as direct promises of this product.

### External observations

- Short-form video is a major format on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, and marketers use it for engagement, product research and sales influence.
- YouTube Shorts has grown into a separate short-video format and research shows creators increased Shorts production after the format appeared.
- Academic analysis of YouTube Shorts found that Shorts generally attract more views and likes per view than regular videos, but not equally across all categories; educational and political categories do not outperform regular videos as strongly.
- Short-video recommendation systems use passive user behavior such as skipping; this supports the importance of first seconds, clarity and retention, but should be framed as market context.
- There is a risk zone around clickbait and deceptive hooks. Future copy should distinguish strong hooks from misleading clickbait.

## Competitor/SERP opportunities

### Opportunity 1 — avoid generic “go viral” advice

Many viral-video articles tend to give generic advice: follow trends, make hooks, post consistently. The Goodly review page can be stronger if it explains which parts of the PDF cover formulas, retention, repackaging and the viewer path.

### Opportunity 2 — separate attention mechanics from deception

The page should explicitly frame hooks as relevance and clarity, not clickbait. This matters because clickbait research and platform policies make deceptive attention tactics risky.

### Opportunity 3 — connect content and business use

The product page verifies a path from video views to applications or purchases. This can be a differentiator versus pages that stop at views and likes.

### Opportunity 4 — explain resale and lead-magnet use clearly

Many users may not understand resale rights. A simple explanation block can improve trust and reduce confusion.
